How to delete your SwipeFlipper account

You can request deletion of your SwipeFlipper account and all associated data at any time.

  1. Send an email to:
    admin@getcharityswipe.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete my SwipeFlipper account"
  3. Send the email from the address associated with your SwipeFlipper account (or include it in the body)
  4. We will confirm receipt within 3 working days
  5. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days

What data will be deleted

  • Your email address and account credentials
  • Your display name (if you provided one)
  • All scanned items and their details
  • All photos associated with your scans
  • Your listings (Drafts, Listed, Sold)
  • Your profit tracking history
  • All other data associated with your account

What data may be retained

In limited circumstances, we may retain some data for legal or compliance reasons:

  • Anonymised data: Aggregated statistics that no longer identify you may be retained indefinitely
  • Legal obligations: Data required to be retained by law (e.g., tax records if applicable)
  • Fraud prevention: Data needed to prevent fraud or abuse

Questions?

If you have any questions about deleting your account or data, please contact us at admin@getcharityswipe.com.
