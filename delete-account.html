Delete your account

You can permanently delete your SwipeFlipper account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SwipeFlipper and tap your profile icon on the Home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

  1. Email admin@swipeflipper.co.uk with the subject "Delete my SwipeFlipper account".
  2. Send it from the email address on your account, or include that address in the message.
  3. We will confirm receipt within 3 working days and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, password and display name
  • All scanned items, including scan dates, shop names and locations
  • All listings (drafts, listed and sold) and profit history
  • Your profile and settings

Photos are never stored on our servers, so there are none to delete.

What may be kept

  • Anonymised, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you
  • Records we are legally required to keep (for example tax records for Pro subscriptions), only for as long as the law requires

Pro subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Questions?
