Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the SwipeFlipper mobile app ("SwipeFlipper", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect your email, display name and the items you scan so the app can work. Location is optional. Photos are sent to OpenAI only to identify items and are not stored on our servers. We never sell your data. You can delete your account and all its data in the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account information

Your email address and the display name you choose when you sign up. Used to create and secure your account.

Scan data

Details of the items you scan: title, category, estimated value, purchase and listing prices, status (Scanned, Selected, Rejected, Purchased, Listed, Sold), the date and time of each scan, and the shop name you enter (optional).

Location (optional)

If you allow location access, the app records the GPS position of each scan and a street/area name (for example "Park Street, Bristol") so you can see which shops you found items in. Location is collected only while you are using the app, never in the background. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings; the app works without it. Shops you name are also remembered on your device to suggest them next time.

Photos

Photos you take when scanning are sent to OpenAI to identify the items and estimate their value. They are processed only to produce that result and are not stored on our servers. A copy may stay on your own device while you review the scan.

Purchases

If you subscribe to SwipeFlipper Pro, Google Play handles the payment. We receive only your subscription status, never your card details.

Device and advertising data

On the free tier, Google AdMob shows ads and collects device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions and diagnostics, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads may be personalised.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: identifying items, estimating prices, tracking your items and profits, and showing where and when you scanned them.
  • To manage your account and Pro subscription.
  • To show ads on the free tier.
  • To keep the service secure and comply with the law.

3. Third-party services

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and database. Firebase privacy
  • OpenAI: identifying items from photos. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models. OpenAI privacy policy
  • eBay: looking up current prices. Only item titles and categories are sent, never personal data.
  • Google AdMob: advertising on the free tier. How Google uses data in ads
  • Google Play: app distribution and subscription billing. Google privacy policy
  • Your phone's location services (Google on Android): turning GPS coordinates into a street/area name.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data retention

  • Account and scan data, including location: kept while your account is active. Deleted when you delete an item or your account.
  • Photos: not stored on our servers.
  • Anonymised, aggregated statistics: may be kept indefinitely; they cannot identify you.
  • Data we are legally required to keep: only as long as the law requires.

5. Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Delete account and confirm with your password. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, scanned items, listings, saved locations and profile. You can also request deletion by email; see Delete your account.

6. Your rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent (for example to location). Contact admin@swipeflipper.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Children

SwipeFlipper is not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the UK/EU). If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Some providers (such as OpenAI and Google) process data in the United States. These transfers rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules so only you can read your items. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data, and will notify you and the authorities of any breach as the law requires.

10. California residents

You have the right to know about and delete your personal information and to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information. Contact us to make a request.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
